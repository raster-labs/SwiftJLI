## Detailed Findings and Documented Boundaries

### 1. Repaired `HighBit` (`(0028,0102)`) Bit-Shift Alignment in Retained DICOM Test Support
- **Finding**: As recorded in [`codex-inherited-support-comparison.json`](codex-inherited-support-comparison.json) (captured at baseline before the `DICOMReader.swift` edit by the historical live-checkout comparison script [`compare_support.py`](compare_support.py) across `JLISwift` `fb1e188b4b1bad73febccf3b7c7595a78ae7a4c2` and `SwiftJLI` `44e3807bd32d45ceca74404297eded4fd30a746f`), `Tests/SwiftJLITests/Support/DICOMReader.swift`, `DICOMWriter.swift`, and `DICOMWindowRenderer.swift` were algorithmically identical to `JLISwift` at `fb1e188b4b1bad73febccf3b7c7595a78ae7a4c2` after removing `public` modifiers and the `Accelerate` conditional wrapper. However, in both `fb1e188b4b1bad73febccf3b7c7595a78ae7a4c2` and baseline `SwiftJLI` (`DICOMReader.swift` SHA-256 `d5a6afc113277eb58b9cb5c979231b971b4a63ff00ca81140f71935bc261b435`, retained in [`baseline-support/DICOMReader.swift`](baseline-support/DICOMReader.swift) and [`baseline-support-sha256.json`](baseline-support-sha256.json)), `DICOMImage.decodeIntensities()` parsed `highBit` (`(0028,0102)`) and documented honouring `bitsStored`/`highBit`, yet only masked the low `bitsStored` bits (`raw & mask`). (Note: [`compare_support.py`](compare_support.py) reads the live working trees and overwrites `codex-inherited-support-comparison.json`, so it is retained as the historical baseline-capture script rather than rerun after editing `DICOMReader.swift`.)
- **Standard vs Legacy Distinction**: Current [NEMA PS3.5 §8.1.1](https://dicom.nema.org/medical/dicom/current/output/chtml/part05/chapter_8.html) requires canonical low-bit alignment (`HighBit == BitsStored - 1`, shift `0`), which was already handled accurately (`12 / 12` canonical cases passed in [`dicom-normal-value-oracle.swift`](dicom-normal-value-oracle.swift) at baseline). Current PS3.5 §8.1.1 Note 3 points to PS3.5 2014c for the former general-case rule allowing other `HighBit` positions within `BitsAllocated` (`BitsStored - 1 <= HighBit < BitsAllocated`). Repairing shifted-sample extraction in `DICOMReader` is therefore a legacy compatibility fix for non-canonical `HighBit` positions rather than a correction to modern canonical DICOM samples.
- **Fix**: `DICOMImage.decodeIntensities()` (`Tests/SwiftJLITests/Support/DICOMReader.swift#L211-L258`, SHA-256 `227a4f1b3f42c527c5bd7fcf457be0483fe8e6cf9aaf07397adf2bc9edd8cc74`, retained in [`candidate-support/DICOMReader.swift`](candidate-support/DICOMReader.swift) and [`candidate-support-sha256.json`](candidate-support-sha256.json)) now computes `shift = (highBit >= stored - 1 && highBit < bitsAllocated) ? UInt32(highBit - (stored - 1)) : 0` and extracts `(raw >> shift) & mask` before two's-complement sign extension from bit `stored - 1`.
- **Verification & Reproduction**:
  - Codex's independent 56-case normal-value harness ([`dicom-normal-value-oracle.swift`](dicom-normal-value-oracle.swift), SHA-256 `8954004ec2fa30f06ad9993193142094bdd68a009a3816c9f63e5a98a373bf1c`) improved from `12 / 56` passed (`44` shifted-layout mismatches, `0` canonical failures) at baseline ([`codex-normal-values-baseline.json`](codex-normal-values-baseline.json), [`codex-normal-values-baseline.log`](codex-normal-values-baseline.log)) to **`56 / 56` passed (`0` failures)** ([`codex-normal-values-candidate.json`](codex-normal-values-candidate.json), [`codex-normal-values-candidate.log`](codex-normal-values-candidate.log)). Both baseline compile attempts (initial sandbox module-cache failure [`codex-normal-values-baseline-build.json`](codex-normal-values-baseline-build.json) and successful `-module-cache-path` retry [`codex-normal-values-baseline-build-retry.json`](codex-normal-values-baseline-build-retry.json)) and the candidate build ([`codex-normal-values-candidate-build.json`](codex-normal-values-candidate-build.json)) are retained.

### 2. Expanded Normal Conformance Coverage in `Tests/SwiftJLITests/Native/`
1. **Recursive Nested Undefined-Length `SQ` Skipping (`DICOMReaderTests.nestedUndefinedLengthSequenceIsSkipped`, `Tests/SwiftJLITests/Native/DICOMReaderTests.swift#L244-L286`)**:
   - Places the real group-`0028` pixel-module attributes (`2×2`, `RescaleSlope = 2`, `RescaleIntercept = -10`) *before* an outer undefined-length sequence `(0028,3010)` containing an inner undefined-length sequence `(0040,0555)` and post-inner sibling decoy group-`0028` elements (`1×2` and `2×1` geometry, decoy slope/intercept), followed by `PixelData` `(7FE0,0010)`. Because `DICOMReader.read` scans sequentially until `PixelData`, any leak from either the inner sequence or the sibling elements after the inner `E0DD` delimiter would overwrite the real `2×2` geometry and rescale parameters.
2. **Multi-Fragment Basic Offset Table (`BOT`) Grouping (`DICOMWriterTests.encapsulatedMultiFrameMultiFragmentBOTGrouping`, `Tests/SwiftJLITests/Native/DICOMWriterTests.swift#L322-L409`)**:

### 3. Predecessor Limitations and Architectural Boundaries Preserved Explicitly
- **Public JPEG Boundaries on `SOF3`, Restart, and Multi-Scan Streams (Rows `5.1` & `6.1`)**:
  - `JPEGEnvelope.validate` (`Sources/SwiftJLI/JPEGEnvelope.swift#L57-L78`) explicitly rejects `DRI` (`0xDD`) or `DQT` (`0xDB`) redefinitions after the first `SOS` and rejects multiple sequential scans with `CodecError(.unsupportedFeature)`.
  - Public 3-component lossless (`SOF3`) colour requires explicit ASCII `'R'`, `'G'`, `'B'` component identifiers `[0x52, 0x47, 0x42]` and rejects ambiguous `[1, 2, 3]` lossless colour IDs (`JPEGCodec.info`, `Sources/SwiftJLI/JPEGCodec.swift#L291-L295`). Non-interleaved or subsampled lossless scans are also rejected.
  - Standalone public JPEG (`JPEGCodec.layout`, `Sources/SwiftJLI/JPEGCodec.swift#L72-L75`) rejects signed integer `ImageDescriptor`s (`ImageDescriptor.sampleType == .signedInteger`) because ITU-T T.81 JPEG has no signed-sample marker; retained `DICOMReader`/`DICOMWriter` handle signed 16-bit container samples (`pixelRepresentation == 1`), and internal `JLIImage` lossless tests (`MedicalSafetyTests`, `ConformanceEvidenceTests.c4Signed`) round-trip raw 16-bit bit patterns.
  - Predecessor-specific raw caller-workspace entry points introduced in `JLISwift 0.6.0` (`decodeBaselineRGBCore`, `decodeLosslessMonochromeCore`, `JLICoreOutcome`) are not part of `SwiftJLI`'s common contract API (`ImageDestination` / `CopyPolicy`) and are not claimed as equivalent.
- **Native Multi-Frame Truncation Clamping (Row `1.4`)**: Single-frame native `PixelData` shorter than `frameBytes` throws `DICOMError.truncatedPixelData` (`DICOMReaderTests.truncatedPixelDataIsRejected`). For multi-frame native datasets containing at least one full frame, `DICOMReader` clamps `numberOfFrames` to `max(1, nativeAllFrames.count / frameBytes)` (`DICOMWriterTests.lyingFrameCountClamped`) rather than rejecting every multi-frame buffer with trailing truncation. Neither full clinical DICOM qualification nor universal malformed-DICOM safety is claimed.
- **`YBR_FULL_422` Native Sample Layout (Row `3.1`)**: `DICOMImage.render8bit` and `DICOMColorTests.ybr422IsConverted` convert 3-bytes-per-pixel (`samplesPerPixel == 3`) `Y, Cb, Cr` triplets (matching post-decode encapsulated JPEG planes), not packed 2-bytes-per-pixel uncompressed native `YBR_FULL_422` ([NEMA PS3.3 §C.7.6.3](https://dicom.nema.org/medical/DICOM/current/output/chtml/part03/sect_C.7.6.3.html)).
- **Metadata Embedding/Reassembly vs "Merge Metadata-Bearing JPEGs" (Row `7.1`)**: `SwiftJLI` validates, extracts, reassembles (requiring contiguous `1...count` `APP2` `ICC_PROFILE` chunk sequence numbers), and round-trips `APP1` Exif and multi-chunk `APP2` ICC profile metadata (`e5a5a58`, covered by `MetadataTests`, `APITests`, `XYBAPITests`, and `JPEGInspectionTests`). No API for merging multiple metadata-bearing JPEG codestreams exists in either `JLISwift` or `SwiftJLI`, and `JPEGEnvelope.validate` explicitly rejects concatenated JPEG codestreams or trailing non-pad bytes after `EOI` (`CodecError(.malformedInput)`).
- **`abc040f` (`JLILab` GUI vs Library / Test Support, Row `8.1`)**: Modality LUT (`rescaleSlope`/`rescaleIntercept`) before windowing lives in `DICOMReader` (`Tests/SwiftJLITests/Support/`), whereas `JLILab/Sources/Render.swift` (`CGImage` `noneSkipLast` RGBA packing and `CFData` backing retention) and `JLILab/Sources/ImageLoading.swift` belonged to an app-only macOS target in `JLISwift` and are not part of `SwiftJLI`. Shipping `SwiftJLI` tests row-stride padding and explicit `.discardStraightAlpha` through `PlaneDescriptor` and `DCTOptions.alphaPolicy` (`DCTAlphaPolicy`, covered by `ColourInputTests.rgbaUsesSourceStrideAndDiscardsOnlyExplicitAlpha`, `JPEGDecodeParityTests.independentChroma`, `JPEGMigrationTests`).
- **Frozen 9-Case Corpus vs 71-Record Identity Fixture Dimensions & Synthetic Provenance (Row `10.1`)**:
  - `RegressionCorpusTests.cases` (`Tests/SwiftJLITests/Native/RegressionCorpusTests.swift#L86-L98`) defines nine small deterministic synthetic cases: `ct-16s` (`24x24`), `ct-12s` (`20x28`), `dx-12u` (`32x16`), `mg-12u` (`30x30`), `mr-12u` (`16x16`), `us-8rgb` (`12x16`), `xa-12u` (`28x20`), `sc-8u` (`8x8`), and `ct-16s-lossy-reject` (`8x8`); dimensions here are rows x columns. Their source SHA-256 values are frozen in the same file.
  - The separate 71-record identity matrix in `IdentityHashes.swift` (`Tests/SwiftJLITests/Support/IdentityHashes.swift#L38-L158`) uses `256x256` RGB/greyscale images, a `67x61` RGB image, `256x256` and `512x512` 12/16-bit greyscale images, and `64x64` 12-bit colour input, with additional scaled-decode outputs. `predecessorIdentityMatrixIsRetained` (`Tests/SwiftJLITests/IdentityRegressionTests.swift`) checks the 71-record baseline and seven explicit centred-chroma corrections.
  - Both sets are deterministic synthetic generators containing **zero real patient data (`No PHI`)**; historical `JLISwift` benchmark or clinical prose is not claimed as real-patient dataset validation.
